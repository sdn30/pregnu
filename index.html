Our Story

The Journey Behind
the Vision

Everything we offer today is rooted in the extensive professional journey of Dr. Niyi Adeleye — Real Estate Developer, Project Manager, and Wealth Coach.

His expertise was forged through seven years of private sector experience, leading to his full transition into entrepreneurship in 2006. Between 2006 and 2022, this vision evolved from marketing over 1,200 plots of land as a realtor, to formalising operations, addressing the industry's biggest barrier of financing, and ultimately consolidating into an international real estate group.

"Everything we offer is built on real experience: land, housing, systems, investors, projects, and long-term thinking — not noise."

That is why the Perfection Real Estate Wealth Club exists — to give you a structured pathway to build wealth through real estate, starting from the level your current income can conveniently support.

2006
Transitioned into Full-Time Real Estate
Marketing over 1,200 plots of land across multiple estate projects as a realtor, building foundational expertise in land acquisition and customer relationships.
2010
Incorporated Perfection Assets & Global Resources Ltd
Formalising operations across real estate development, project management, and property marketing under a structured corporate vehicle.
2012
Co-Founded a Creative Real Estate Investment Club
Addressing the industry's biggest barrier — financing — through a member-driven investment approach registered as Perfection Real Estate Investors CMS Ltd.
2016
Launched the Perfection Estate Concept
A platform for land acquisition and banking, home ownership, and housing project financing — a stronger, more organised pathway for real estate participation.
2019
Partnership with BETONIQ — Mass Housing Technology
Partnered with German firm BETONIQ to introduce mass housing technology into Nigeria, launching the mass housing scheme at Perfection Estate Phase 1, Ibeju-Lekki.
2021
Perfection Real Estate Group of Companies Ltd
Formal consolidation of all real estate business outfits into one unified group, incorporated in May 2021.
2022
Expansion into International Real Estate Markets
Launched international investment schemes including Dubai real estate and other global destinations — bringing world-class opportunities directly to members.
